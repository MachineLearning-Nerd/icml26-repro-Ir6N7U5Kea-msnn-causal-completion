# Claim 3 method

The proof certificate specializes Theorem 4.8's MSNN leading term to
`d_max`, then exactly divides the sparse SNN and sparse MSNN terms by that
rich-treatment term. Laurent-monomial exponent vectors expose all cancellations.

An independent implementation reconstructs both ratios and checks exact rational
substitutions. The negative control adds one extra power of `p_d` to the sparse
MSNN term; only the MSNN ratio must fail, which diagnoses the intended error.
