# Claim 2 limitations and deviations

- The certificate verifies the corollary conditional on the two leading-term
  expressions stated in Theorem 4.8; it is not a re-proof of Theorem 4.8.
- The equality is asymptotic. No finite MCAR sample is presented as proof.
- The previous 11.5x/5.6x/1.4x feasible-indicator ratios are rejected as
  evidence for this claim because they are not `K_MSNN/K_SNN` and differ by
  orders of magnitude from the claimed expression.
